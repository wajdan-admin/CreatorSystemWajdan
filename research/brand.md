# Wajdan: the brand truth

**This file travels to every Wajdan system.** Any new session, any new creator account, any second
system. It answers "who are we" so nothing downstream has to guess.

Copy this file across. Do not copy `creators.md`, which is niche research and only valid for the
niche it was built in.

---

## Who we are

Wajdan is an international digital marketing agency based in Budapest, Hungary.

We build client acquisition systems for consultancy firms. Ads, funnel, booking, follow up, all of
it as one machine rather than a list of services.

**Where the clients are:** roughly 70% consultancies and law firms across the UAE, Europe and Asia.
Roughly 30% agencies in Pakistan.

**Who records:** Wajahat. He is the founder and the face. Everything written here has to be
sayable by him in one take.

**Who runs the system:** Danyal.

## The three funnels

| Property | Audience | Use it when |

**The known problem:** the Instagram audience comments in Roman Urdu, which means it skews
Pakistani, but the bio has historically pointed at the UAE funnel. `founders.wajdan.co` has never
been linked from Instagram at all. Whoever picks this up should decide which audience the account
is actually for before optimising anything else.

## What we actually sell

Not leads. Qualified calls that show up.

The positioning line that survived every round of testing:

> Anyone can get you leads. We get you qualified calls.

The distinction is the whole business. A full calendar is not a good calendar. Most of our content
is some version of that one idea.

## Proof we are allowed to use

- **$240,000 generated for a client.** That figure covers **three months**, not one month. Saying
  it any other way is a false claim and has nearly happened more than once.
- **Hainan Corporate Services** is a real case study. Naming them publicly needs their approval
  first. Until that approval exists, describe the result without the name.
- Immigration consultancy result, nineteen consultations a week. This is ours, not from an ad
  library. Never attach an invented link to it.

**Hard rule: never invent a performance number.** Real named case studies are allowed. Invented
ones never are, no matter how illustrative. If a reel needs a number we do not have, use a round
parable number and make it obviously a model, the way the coffee stand example works.

## How Wajahat wants things written

These came from many rounds of being told off. They are not style preferences, they are rejection
criteria.

| Rule | What it means |
|---|---|
| **Complete sentences** | Short does not mean fragmented. Under about twelve words, but whole |
| **No jargon** | "Vertical" got a reel killed. If a normal business owner would not say it, cut it |
| **Never define a word** | If a word needs defining inside the reel, it is the wrong word |
| **No hyphens in documents** | Anything going to Wajahat as a PDF. No filler sounds either |
| **No system explainers** | Content about how our system works is the lowest performing thing we make. Subject beats mechanism by about ten times |
| **Say it properly** | Do not strip a sentence down until the idea stops arriving. Clarity first, brevity second |
| **The body never sells** | The ask lives in the final line, the end card and the caption. Nowhere else |

## Production constraints

Wajahat records. That sets the ceiling.

- Must be shootable in under an hour. If it needs a day, it is the wrong idea.
- Single shot, phone, no crew is the default and it outperforms produced content.
- Animated comparison reels need no recording at all and carry the highest multiples in the
  research. See `scripts/drafts/UNLOCKED-animations.md`.
- Burned in dynamic captions, always.

## The open decision

Brand account or person account. Hormozi runs 5.3M on the person and 48.5K on Acquisition.com, the
same operation with roughly a 107x gap. Wajdan's single best performing reel was Wajahat on camera.

This has never been settled. A new creator system is probably the moment to settle it.
