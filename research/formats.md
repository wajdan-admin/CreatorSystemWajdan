### Corrected spec
- **Organic education: 60-120s.** Longer is fine when the teaching justifies it.
- **Paid / promo / product demo: 20-45s.**
- Pace and idea density matter, not duration. A 90s reel with four usable points beats
  a 25s reel with one vague one.

## Format 7 — Two-person / duo ⭐ strongly represented in the winners
**Proof:** @soldbyhenrygalasso 1.6M views at 39x median, 67s. @nuve.ceo 1.1M at 42x, 70s.
@flojoe.tv 660K at 20x from 1,800 followers, 45s. @itssarahmemmer 504K at 357x, 130s.
Full list and links in `creators.md`.

**Why duo works:** one person carries the viewer's skepticism. The questioner asks what the
viewer is already thinking, which gives the answer somewhere to land. Solo talking head has
to manufacture that tension; a duo gets it free.

**Roles that work:** interviewer/skeptic - audience surrogate asking the dumb question -
roleplay of a real conversation - two-hander where one sets up and one pays off.

**Honesty line:** an actor may play a dramatized role, clearly framed as such. An actor must
never be presented as a real client, or give a testimonial. That is a fabricated endorsement.
